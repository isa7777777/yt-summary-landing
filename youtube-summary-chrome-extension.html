YouTube Summary Chrome extension

Summarize any YouTube video with AI — right on the video page. Key points, clickable timestamps, 15+ languages.*

Add to Chrome — Free

One click, no tab switching

This Chrome extension adds a small widget directly to the YouTube video page. There's no need to copy the video link or open a separate website — just click Summary while you're on the video, and the AI reads the transcript and gives you the key points in seconds.

It works on any YouTube video with a transcript, detects the video's language automatically, and lets you translate the summary into your own language. Switching tabs doesn't cost you a summary, and you start with 2 free summaries to try it out.

🧩 Works on the page

The widget lives on the YouTube video page. No links to copy, no separate site to visit.

⚡ Instant summaries

Click Summary and the AI returns the key points of the video in seconds.

🎯 Clickable timestamps

Jump to important moments with smart, clickable chapter markers.

🔒 Privacy first

We don't track what you watch. Your viewing history stays on YouTube.

* Summaries are generated from video transcripts. Some YouTube videos may not have transcripts available.

Frequently asked questions

How do I install the extension?

Click "Add to Chrome — Free", which takes you to the Chrome Web Store. Click "Add to Chrome" and the extension is ready in a few seconds.

Which browsers does it support?

It's a Chrome extension and works on Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers such as Edge and Brave.

Is it free?

Yes. You get 2 free summaries to start. Paid plans start at $3.25/month if you need more.
