Frequently asked questions

How do I summarize a YouTube video?

Install the YouTube Summary Chrome extension, open any YouTube video, and click the Summary button. The AI reads the video transcript and gives you a concise summary with clickable timestamps in seconds.

Is YouTube Summary free?

Yes. You get 2 free summaries to start with no payment required. Paid plans start at $3.25/month for 50 summaries per month if you need more.

What languages does it support?

It works with videos in 15+ languages including English, Spanish, German, French, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. It can also translate the summary into your own language with one click.

Does it work on any YouTube video?

It works on any YouTube video that has a transcript available — most videos do. The summary is generated directly from that transcript.

Do you track what I watch?

No. We don't track your viewing history. Your YouTube activity stays on YouTube.
